Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

TapDeck is built to know as little about you as possible. This policy explains the little it does handle. It's run by Saba Burduli, an independent developer.

What stays on your Mac

Your gestures, shortcuts, settings and trackpad data never leave your Mac. TapDeck reads finger positions from the trackpad only to recognise gestures, in memory, and doesn't record or store them. Your settings are one small file in ~/Library/Application Support/TapDeck/.

Your keyboard

So a hand brushing the trackpad while you type doesn't run a shortcut, TapDeck notices when a key is pressed and pauses gestures for half a second. It keeps only the time of your last key press, in memory. It never records which keys you press or what you type, and nothing about your typing leaves your Mac. Set Settings › Sensitivity › Pause after typing to 0 and TapDeck stops listening to the keyboard entirely.

The licence check

When you activate TapDeck, and about once a day after that, the app sends your licence key, an activation ID and your Mac's name (as set in System Settings, for example "Saba's MacBook Pro") to Dodo Payments to confirm the licence is valid. Nothing else is sent. Your key is stored in your Mac's Keychain.

When you buy

Purchases are handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. They collect your name, email address, billing address and payment details to process the payment, calculate tax, and send your receipt and licence key. We can see your name, email and purchase details in our dashboard so we can help you with your licence. We never see your full card details. Dodo Payments' own privacy policy covers how they handle your data.

What we don't do

No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and we don't sell or share your data. This website doesn't use cookies or tracking scripts.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep purchase records as long as the law requires for tax and accounting. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email us at feedback@tapdeck.tech. You can also delete TapDeck's local data by removing the folder above and the "TapDeck" Keychain item.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
