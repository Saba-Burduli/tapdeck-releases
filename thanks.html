1. Download TapDeck

Download TapDeck

Open the DMG and drag TapDeck to Applications. TapDeck isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open it macOS says it can't check it: open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once; updates install by themselves.

2. Paste your key

Open TapDeck. The Activate window asks for your key: paste it and click Activate. It works on up to 3 Macs.

3. Allow Accessibility

TapDeck then asks for Accessibility access, which it needs to press shortcuts and move windows. That's it: hold three fingers still on your trackpad for a moment, and Mission Control opens. Do a five-finger double tap anytime to see every gesture for the app you're in.
