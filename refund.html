Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

14-day refunds

If TapDeck isn't right for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of your first purchase or of a yearly renewal. No questions asked.

How to ask

Request it from the Dodo Payments customer portal, or reach us through our support page. Please don't post your email or order details there publicly; we'll reply with a private way to continue. Refunds are issued by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show it.

After a refund

The licence key is deactivated and TapDeck stops working on all your Macs.

Cancelling instead

You can cancel renewal at any time in the Dodo Payments customer portal. TapDeck keeps working until the end of the year you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any refund rights you have under the consumer law where you live.
