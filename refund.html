Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Try it free for 3 days

Every TapDeck subscription starts with a 3-day free trial with everything unlocked. You add a card at checkout, but nothing is charged during the trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you're never charged.

After the trial

When the 3 days are up, your card is charged $9 for one year. Because you've had the trial to decide, yearly charges, including renewals, are not refundable. Cancel before a renewal date and you won't be charged again; TapDeck keeps working until the end of the year you've paid for.

How to cancel

Cancel any time in the Dodo Payments customer portal, linked from your receipt email. For help, reach us through our support page. Please don't post your email or order details there publicly; we'll reply with a private way to continue.

Your legal rights

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. Nothing in this policy limits any refund rights you have under Dodo Payments' buyer terms or the consumer law where you live; any refund owed under them is issued by Dodo Payments to the original payment method.
